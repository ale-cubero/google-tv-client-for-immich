TV client for Immich brings your self-hosted Immich photo and video library to Android TV and Google TV, built for the couch and the remote.

BROWSE YOUR WHOLE LIBRARY
• "On this day" memories, Recent, Albums, People, Places and Videos on one Home screen
• A smooth month-by-month timeline, even with tens of thousands of photos, plus a year rail to jump across years
• Smart search: find photos by what's in them ("beach", "birthday cake", "dog") using your server's smart search

ENJOY IT ON THE BIG SCREEN
• Full-screen photo and video viewer with hardware-accelerated playback and HDR
• Motion Photos (live photos) play on demand
• Photo details: date, place, camera, exposure and people
• Slideshow from any photo, and an optional screensaver with photos from your library

MADE FOR THE REMOTE
• Every screen works with just the D-pad, OK and Back
• Light and dark themes, three text sizes and three Home thumbnail sizes
• English, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian

READ-ONLY AND PRIVATE
• The app can only view your library. It can never upload, delete, edit or favorite anything on your server.
• It talks only to the server you set up. No analytics, ads or tracking.
• Your API key is encrypted on the device.
• Servers with a self-signed certificate are supported: you review the certificate once and trust exactly that one.

WHAT YOU NEED
• Your own Immich server (tested with Immich v3.x) that your TV can reach
• An API key from Immich (Account Settings → API Keys). A key with read-only permissions is recommended.

This is an unofficial, open-source app (AGPL-3.0). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Immich project.
